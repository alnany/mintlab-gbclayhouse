Frequently Asked Questions

A few of the most frequently asked questions are listed below and separated into categories.

  • Classes I will be out of town for one of the classes. Are there make-up classes or can I participate in another class on another day? No, we do not offer make up classes nor are students able to attend another class. Students are given 40+ hours of open studio access every week which leaves plenty of opportunity for students to practice outside of class. We recommend letting your instructor know before you leave town and they would be happy to fill you in on what you might be missing. I’m trying to get into a class but everything is always sold out. Any tips? Help! We are so sorry if you have not been able to get into a class with us. Our classes do sell out, some classes well out within literal seconds! We recommend setting an alarm/reminder on your phone or marking your calendars in order to sign up as soon as registration opens. Fill out our waitlist form to be automatically added to our mailing list. I wasn’t able to get into a class. Do you have other options available? I’m dying to get my hands dirty. The answer is, YES! Your Raku registration fee includes bisque firing. Please visit the Raku page for additional information and restrictions. What kind of clay can I use for Raku? Anything above cone 5 would be just fine. However, we recommend a clay body that contains sand or grog to withstand the thermal shock. Do my pieces have to be bisqued ahead of time? Yes. All pieces must be bisqued ahead of time. Please no greenware. When can I come in to glaze my pieces? We send out an email to all Raku participants on the Monday before the scheduled Raku firing. The email will contain information on glazing days which are usually on the Friday or Saturday of the firing. Can I bring my own combustibles? Feel free to bring it in. Our Raku Masters will let you know if it’s something you can use. Can I use my own Raku glaze? Yes, of course! Just be sure to apply the glaze at least 24 hours before the firing - the glaze needs to be completely dry. We recommend bringing the glaze with you to our studio on the glazing days before your Raku Firing and doing the glazing in the studio so that you can have it on the shelf ready to go for the Sunday firing. Are your Raku Firings open to the public? Yes, absolutely! We love making new friends :) I’m signed up for a Raku Firing, can I bring a friend? Yes, but only as a spectator. We will not be able to fire their work if they are not signed up.

Raku

  1. Do you offer private Raku firings? Yes, we do! Please email erin@gbclayhouse.com, for more information on booking a private rake firing for yourself or a group of fellow pyromaniacs!
  2. How often do you have Raku firings? Weather permitting, we generally offer Raku firings once a month. Can you bisque my pieces for me before the Raku firing? If so, how much will it cost? YES! Your Raku registration fee includes bisque firing. Please visit the Raku page for additional information and restrictions.
  3. Can you bisque my pieces for me before the Raku firing? If so, how much will it cost? YES! Your Raku registration fee includes bisque firing. Please visit the Raku page for additional information and restrictions.
  4. What kind of clay can I use for Raku? Anything above cone 5 would be just fine. However, we recommend a clay body that contains sand or grog to withstand the thermal shock.
  5. Do my pieces have to be bisqued ahead of time? Yes. All pieces must be bisqued ahead of time. Please no greenware.
  6. When can I come in to glaze my pieces? We send out an email to all Raku participants on the Monday before the scheduled Raku firing. The email will contain information on glazing days which are usually on the Friday or Saturday of the firing. If you can’t make those days, feel free to reach out to us and we can arrange for you to come in on a day that works better for you.
  7. Do you provide horsehair or slip for naked Raku? We sure do!
  8. Can I bring my own combustibles? Feel free to bring it in. Our Raku Masters will let you know if it’s something you can use.
  9. Can I use my own Raku glaze? Yes, of course! Just be sure to apply the glaze at least 24 hours before the firing - the glaze needs to be completely dry. We recommend bringing the glaze with you to our studio on the glazing days before your Raku Firing and doing the glazing in the studio so that you can have it on the shelf ready to go for the Sunday firing.
  10. Are your Raku Firings open to the public? Yes, absolutely! We love making new friends :)
  11. I’m signed up for a Raku Firing, can I bring a friend? Yes, but only as a spectator. We will not be able to fire their work if they are not signed up.
