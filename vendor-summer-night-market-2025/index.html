The 2nd Annual Summer Night Market at Green & Bisque Clayhouse is happening Saturday, June 21st from 4pm to 9pm and we’re inviting you to be a part of it. Through this collection, we honor the people, the places, and the quiet strength that endures long after the flames have passed. We can’t wait to gather with you again—this time, with even deeper purpose and more love than ever.

Summer is almost here, and so is one of the most meaningful events we’ve ever hosted. The 2nd Annual Summer Night Market at Green & Bisque Clayhouse is happening Saturday, June 21st from 4pm to 9pm and we’re inviting you to be a part of it.

This year’s event is more than a market—it’s a tribute to the strength, creativity, and resilience of our community in the wake of the recent fires. Vendor participation will once again be offered exclusively to our studio members, instructors, and staff, with priority registration for those who have volunteered or contributed to our fire relief efforts.

The event will take place both indoors and outdoors. In addition to the vendor booths, we’ll feature an exciting ceramic competition, themed around mythological creatures of fire, water, and rebirth . Entrants will compete for a cash prize, and the energy of the evening promises to be vibrant, heartfelt, and unforgettable. Echoes of the Flame is a special exhibition held in conjunction with our 2nd Annual Summer Night Market—featuring ceramic pieces that survived the recent fires or were created in response to them. These works, each marked by ash, memory, or intention, tell stories of loss, resilience, and transformation. Through this collection, we honor the people, the places, and the quiet strength that endures long after the flames have passed.

We can’t wait to gather with you again—this time, with even deeper purpose and more love than ever.
