Green & Bisque Clayhouse, formerly known as “Xiem Clay Center”, was founded in 2003 to foster a community of ceramic artists and to further public awareness, understanding, and appreciation of ceramic arts. Our new group of dedicated staff, technicians, and instructors are committed to this vision. We strive to provide an inspiring studio environment and quality ceramics education for all skill levels.
