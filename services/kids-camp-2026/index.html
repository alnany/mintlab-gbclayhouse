Kids Clay Camp!

  • 3 Day Kids Camp Hand-Building Explorers Camp June 16th, 17th, & 18th 10:00 AM - 1:00 PM (all three days) In this 3-day adventure for kids ages 5-8, young artists will discover the joy of shaping clay with their hands!
