Wheel Weekday at Green & Bisque Clayhouse

  • What's "Wheel Weekday"? As many of you might know, our six week session classes fill up within seconds. With hundreds of people on the waiting list every session and very few cancellations, it pains us when we can’t get people into our classes. So! We came up with….Wheel Weekday! It’s a great option for anyone who is itching to get their hands in some clay but weren’t able to get into a 6-week class and also for those who are not able to commit to a 6-week course but have always wanted to learn the art of pottery. In this 2-hour group pottery class, participants will learn how to make a cup/bowl on the pottery wheel with guided hands-on instruction. After you've made your bowls, you'll choose the glaze option for the piece you made. Your pottery will then be fired and glazed. We will call you 4-5 weeks after your class when your pottery is ready for pick up.
  • Who can attend? Anyone 16+ years and up!
  • How much is it? $80 per person for two hours of wheel throwing! Participants can make up to two pieces. Participants will choose from a selection of our beautiful glazes at the end of the class. Leave the rest to us! We will call you in 4-5 weeks when your pottery is ready for pick up. We thank you in advance for your understanding. Ceramics is a multi-step process and requires a little patience!
