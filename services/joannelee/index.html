  • Workshop Description Join us for a three-day hand-building workshop exploring coiling as both a meditative practice and a precise construction method. Visiting artist Joanne Seongweon Lee will guide participants through techniques for rolling thin, consistent coils and building refined, structurally sound forms. Beginning with foundational vessels such as cups and cylinders, students will gradually expand into bowls, domed forms, and ultimately a Moon Jar. Through live demonstrations and focused hands-on practice, participants will deepen their understanding of structure, proportion, and surface while strengthening control and intention in their making. This workshop is well-suited for students with some hand-building experience who are ready to refine their skills and explore coil construction at a higher level. About the Artist: Joanne Seongweon Lee

Originally from South Korea, Joanne Seongweon Lee received her BFA in Ceramics from the New York State College of Ceramics at Alfred University in 2015. Following graduation, she returned to South Korea to participate in the Artist in Residence Program at the Korea Ceramics Foundation: Icheon Cerapia. She completed her MFA at Cranbrook Academy of Art in 2019, where she was awarded the Franz Rising Star Project Scholarship for Outstanding Performance in Porcelain Design.

After completing a residency at Sonoma Ceramics in Sonoma, California, Joanne taught at James Madison University in Harrisonburg, Virginia from 2021–2022. She currently serves as the Ceramics Coordinator at Anderson Ranch Arts Center in Snowmass Village, Colorado.
