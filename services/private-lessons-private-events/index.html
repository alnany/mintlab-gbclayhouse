Private Lessons & Group Events

Roll up your sleeves, it’s time to get muddy!

Your instructor will begin by demonstrating the mesmerizing process of throwing a basic form on the wheel. Then it will be your turn to get your hands into the clay and create your own masterpieces. Anything you want to keep will be glazed available for you to pick up when it’s ready. Complimentary local delivery available. Turn around time is about 3-5 weeks.

5-Lesson Private Package: A Comprehensive Ceramics Journey

Immerse yourself in a series of five 1-hour private lessons, tailored to your skill level and interests. Each lesson comes with full-day studio access, giving you ample time to practice and refine your techniques.

Package Details:

  • Lesson Duration: Five 1-hour private lessons

  • Work Limit: Create up to 1,200 cubic inches of work (calculated as width x length x height)
