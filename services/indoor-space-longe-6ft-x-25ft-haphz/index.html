Green & Bisque Holiday Sale – Vendor Information

Thank you for reserving a space at the Green & Bisque Holiday Sale! We’re excited to have you join us as a vendor.

  • This is one table space (one 6 ft. table).
  • Please bring your own tablecloths, props, and any other items you’d like to use for displaying your ceramic pieces.
  • Out of consideration for safety and foot traffic, please avoid bringing overly large pieces. No items may be placed on the floor.
  • If you plan to hang work, please bring your own hanging mechanisms.
  • Artists are responsible for processing their own sales.
  • Saturday, November 29 – 10 AM to 5 PM
  • Sunday, November 30 – 10 AM to 5 PM
  • Location: Green & Bisque Clayhouse, 1563 N. Lake Ave, Pasadena, CA 91104
  • Mandatory Orientation : Before the sale, we’ll host an orientation covering the sales system, setup details, and other key information. Artists will manage their own sales using Square, Venmo, Zelle, or PayPal. The orientation date and time will be announced soon.
  • Mandatory Setup & Cleaning Day : Friday, November 28 at 11 AM. This is a community effort to transform the studio space for the event. All booths must be fully set up by 8 PM that evening.
  • Sales Commission : Green & Bisque Clayhouse will retain 20% of all sales made during the event.

We deeply appreciate your participation and cooperation in helping make this event a success. Thank you—we can’t wait to celebrate with you!
