Price & Information on our Six Week Courses

$385 (Returning students receive a $25 discount)

Each class meets once a week for six weeks and includes two hours of instruction, featuring demonstrations and hands-on guidance from your instructor.

In addition to weekly classes, students have a two-week grace period at the end of the session to finish trimming, glazing, and other work—totaling 8 weeks of studio access.

Open studio hours are available throughout the 6-week instructional period:

  • Monday through Friday, 10 AM – 9 PM
  • Saturday & Sunday, 10 AM – 5 PM

This provides ample opportunity to practice outside of class time.

Students may produce up to 3,000 cubic inches of work (calculated as width × length × height). Independent Study students may produce up to 3,500 cubic inches. Additional inches may be purchased in the shop: either $50 for 1,500 cubic inches, or à la carte at $0.10 per cubic inch.

Your first 25 lb bag of clay is included with registration.

All classes are held in our newly renovated front studio, with additional access to the main studio during designated lab hours and for glazing.

A Note About Learning Ceramics

Ceramics is a craft that rewards time, patience, and consistent practice. While our classes provide the guidance, techniques, and support you need to build a strong foundation, mastery does not happen in a single session or even a single course.

If your goal is to see significant improvement, we encourage you to spend time practicing outside of class. The students who progress the fastest are the ones who return to the studio, repeat the fundamentals, experiment, make mistakes, and keep creating.

Every potter you admire has made hundreds, if not thousands, of pieces along the way. Embrace the process, celebrate your progress, and remember that every piece you make is another step in your journey.

1. I will be out of town for one of the classes. Are there make-up classes or can I participate in another class on a different day?

Unfortunately, we do not offer make-up classes or allow students to attend another class on a different day. However, you’ll have access to over 40 hours of open studio time each week to practice outside of class. We recommend notifying your instructor in advance so they can help you catch up on anything you might miss.

2. I’m trying to get into a class, but everything is always sold out. Any tips?

We understand the frustration! Our classes do sell out quickly—some within seconds. To improve your chances, we suggest setting a reminder or alarm to register as soon as sign-ups open. You can also fill out our waitlist form to be added to our mailing list and receive early notifications.

3. What are the studio access hours for students?

  • Monday - Friday (week 1-6): 10 AM - 9 PM
  • Monday - Friday (grace period: week 7 & 8): 10 AM - 6 PM
  • Saturday & Sunday: 10 AM - 5 PM

4. I have a gift card. Can I use it for a 6-week class?

Yes! Please give us a call at the studio and we’ll be happy to get you enrolled using your gift card. We apologize for the extra step, but this is the only way we can apply gift cards to classes.

5. I tried to register for my friend, but now the class is full!

To ensure you and your friend can get into the same class, we recommend both of you enrolling at the same time on your own devices as soon as registration opens. Spots fill quickly, and it’s best to act fast!
