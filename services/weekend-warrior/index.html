Weekend Warrior at Green & Bisque Clayhouse

  • What's "Weekend Warrior"? Can't commit to a six-week class but itching to get your hands in some clay? Weekend Warriors is the perfect introduction to pottery. In this 2.5-hour workshop, you'll get to experience both of the primary ways clay artists create: wheel throwing and hand-building. With guidance from one of our experienced instructors, you'll learn the fundamentals of each technique and create two unique pieces of your own, one on the pottery wheel and one by hand. Whether you've always wanted to try pottery, couldn't snag a spot in one of our six-week courses, or simply want a creative weekend activity with friends, Weekend Warriors offers a fun, approachable way to explore the art of ceramics without the long-term commitment. After class, you'll select a glaze for your pieces and leave the rest to us. Our team will carefully fire and finish your work, and we'll notify you when it's ready for pickup approximately 4–5 weeks later. No experience necessary. Just bring your curiosity and be prepared to get a little muddy.
  • Who can attend? Anyone 16+ years and up!
  • How much is it? $80 per person for two hours of wheel throwing! Participants can make up to two pieces. Participants will choose from a selection of our beautiful glazes at the end of the class. Leave the rest to us! We will call you in 4-5 weeks when your pottery is ready for pick up. We thank you in advance for your understanding. Ceramics is a multi-step process and requires a little patience!
